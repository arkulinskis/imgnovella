Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything Ren'Py does with scripts (characters, menus, jumps, variables, conditions), ImgNovella does with forms and a map.

Is it free?

Yes. The Free plan includes every editor feature, publishing and exporting. Pictures are kept to 400 × 600 px and you can have 5 novels. Pro allows 1200 × 1800 pictures, 100 novels and removes the ImgNovella credit from exported games.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes. "Package & sell" downloads your novel as a standalone HTML game (or an itch.io-ready zip). Sell it wherever you like; we take no cut. Only use art you have the rights to: the built-in starter art is free to use.

Do readers need an account?

No. Anyone can play published novels. Their saves, endings and settings stay in their own browser. An account is only needed to like novels or to create.

Who can see my novel?

Only you, until you publish. Public novels appear in Discover; unlisted ones only play for people with the link. Publishing saves a copy, so you can keep editing in private.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, for reading and for writing. Novels can be portrait (made for phones) or landscape.
