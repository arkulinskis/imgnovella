The basics

Scenes

A novel is a set of scenes on a map. Each scene is a list of steps (lines, backgrounds, characters…) followed by choices.

Choices

Choices connect scenes. The map draws them as arrows, so you can see every branch at a glance.

Endings

Mark final scenes as endings (true, good, neutral, bad or secret). Readers collect them on the Endings screen.

Variables

Numbers, yes/no flags and text that remember what the reader did, so later scenes can react.

Choices & branches

Conditional choices

Give a choice a condition. When it isn't met, hide the choice or show it locked with a hint ("Needs more courage"): readers learn what they missed.

Effects

A choice can change variables when picked: courage + 1, met_mira = true.

Once-only choices

Tick "only once" and loop back to the same scene: a menu of questions that empties as the reader asks them.

Timed choices

Give a scene a timer. If the reader hesitates, the choice you picked is made for them.

Small differences

You don't need a whole new scene for a small change: give any line a condition, or use {if …}…{else}…{/if} inside a line.

Story check

The Check button finds choices that lead nowhere, scenes nobody can reach, dead ends and typos in conditions.

Mini-games

Add a Mini-game step anywhere in a scene. Pick a game, a difficulty and three colours (main, accent and panel) so it matches your novel, then choose what happens next.

Win or lose paths

"If they win" and "If they lose" can each carry on in the scene or lead to another scene. The map draws them as dashed mint arrows.

Remember the result

Store the result in a yes / no variable (true if they won), then react later: {if won_duel}You beat me!{else}Better luck next time.{/if}

Retry & skip

Offer "Try again" after a loss, and let readers skip a game (it counts as a loss) so nobody gets stuck.

Difficulty

Easy, Normal or Hard changes speed, time limits and how clever the computer plays.

Music & sounds

Built-in moods

Calm, Romance, Upbeat, Mystery, Tension, Sad, Dreamy and Rain, made in the browser: they cost no space.

Your own files

Upload MP3, OGG, WAV or M4A in the Sounds tab, as music (loops) or sound effects (play once). Free: up to 1 MB per file, 10 per novel. Pro: 4 MB, 60.

In exported games

Your music and sounds are packed into the HTML game, so it plays offline too.

Rights

Only upload audio you made or have a licence for, especially in novels you sell.

What readers get

Title screen

Start, Continue, Load, Endings, Gallery, Story map, Settings and Credits.

Rollback & history

Back (Backspace or scroll up) undoes lines; History shows everything said. "Back to the last choice" after an ending.

Auto & skip

Auto-play, and Skip that only skips text they've already read (or hold Ctrl).

Saves

Six save slots plus autosave, kept on their device. Quick to resume.

Story map

After playing, readers see the scenes they found and can replay from any of them.

Settings

Text speed, auto pace, text size, music and sound volume, full screen. Works with keyboard, mouse and touch.

Sharing & selling

Publish

Public novels appear in Discover; unlisted ones play by link. Publishing freezes a copy, so you can keep editing.

Package & sell

Download your novel as a single HTML file (or an itch.io-ready zip) with every picture inside. Sell it on itch.io, Gumroad, Ko-fi or your own site.

Free & Pro

Free: pictures up to 400 × 600 and 5 novels. Pro: up to 1200 × 1800, 100 novels and no ImgNovella credit on exports.

Starter art

The built-in backgrounds, characters and illustrations are free to use in any novel, including ones you sell.
